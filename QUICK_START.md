# AppForge Quick Start Guide

This guide walks you through creating and deploying your first ServiceNow application in **under 15 minutes**.

---

## 1. Open the AppForge Studio Workspace
In your ServiceNow browser window, navigate to:  
👉 **`https://<your-instance>.service-now.com/x_1805046_app_fo_0_workspace.do`**

Or type **`App-forge`** in the left filter navigator.

---

## 2. Create Your First Application
1. Click **⚡ Create Application** from the sidebar.
2. Select a template (e.g., **Employee Onboarding**).
3. Enter your **Application Name** and **Business Owner**.
4. Click **Create Application 🚀**.

AppForge automatically provisions physical database tables, fields, business rules, and security ACLs in seconds.

---

## 3. Review & Validate
1. Go to **📦 My Applications** to view your newly created application.
2. Verify:
   * **Active Version:** `v1.0.0`
   * **Package Integrity:** `✓ Signed & Trusted`
   * **Security Gate:** `Passed (Four-Eyes)`
   * **Schema Drift:** `None`

---

## 4. One-Click Deployment
1. Click **Deploy 🚀**.
2. Review the automated preflight checklist.
3. Click **Deploy to Production 🚀**.

Your application is now live, certified, and accessible to end users in your ServiceNow instance!

# AppForge Administrator Marketplace Guide

Covers template management, customer provisioning, entitlements, and governed application releases.

---

## 🧭 Administrator Responsibilities
* **Publishing Templates:** Author and certify 5-layer declarative templates.
* **Managing Customers & Tenants:** Bind multi-tenant instances to customer CRM accounts.
* **Pricing & Subscriptions:** Configure pricing models (SUBSCRIPTION, PER_USER, PER_APPLICATION, ENTERPRISE).
* **Release Approval:** Review preflight validation and provide Four-Eyes cryptographic signatures (`POL-SEC-006`).
